Lesson 01 • Fundamentals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Define an electric motor and explain its energy conversion.
  • Identify the stator, rotor, shaft, bearings and cooling fan.
  • Distinguish common AC and DC motor types.
  • Use basic speed, power and torque formulas.
  • Recognize essential motor safety practices.

  • Stator, rotor, shaft, bearing fi fan adda baasuu.

01 • Working principle

What is an electric motor? / Mootarri elektirikaa maali?

An electric motor is a machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. It produces rotation or another form of motion to drive pumps, fans, conveyors, compressors and machine tools.

In many motors, a magnetic field interacts with electric current in conductors. This interaction creates force and torque (turning effect) on the rotor.

Mootarri elektirikaa maashina anniisaa elektirikaa gara anniisaa makaanikaatti jijjiirudha. Paampii, fan, konveeyarii, kompreesara fi meeshaalee warshaa sochoosa.

Mootaroota hedduu keessatti dirreen maagneetii yaa'a elektirikaa kondaktara keessa darbu waliin walitti dhufuun humnaa fi torque (humna naannessuu) rotor irratti uuma.

Electrical input → motor → mechanical output / Galtee elektirikaa → mootara → ba'aa makaanikaa
Key idea / Yaad-rimee ijoo: A motor converts energy; it does not create energy. Some input energy is lost as heat, sound and friction. / Mootarri anniisaa jijjiira malee hin uumu. Anniisaan galchaa keessaa muraasni ho'a, sagalee fi walitti bu'iinsaan bada.

04 • Essential formulas

Motor formulas / Foormulaawwan mootaraa

1. Synchronous speed / Saffisa synchronous

Ns = (120 × f) / P

Ns = synchronous speed (rpm); f = frequency (Hz); P = number of poles.

Ns = saffisa synchronous (rpm); f = firikuweensii (Hz); P = baay'ina poolii.

2. Slip of an induction motor / Slip mootara induction

s = [(Ns − Nr) / Ns] × 100%

Nr = rotor speed (rpm). Slip is the difference between synchronous speed and rotor speed as a percentage of synchronous speed.

Nr saffisa rotor dha. Slip jechuun garaagarummaa saffisa synchronous fi rotor dhibbeentaadhaan ibsame.

3. Mechanical output power / Humna makaanikaa ba'aa

P = (2π × N × T) / 60

P = power (W); N = rotational speed (rpm); T = torque (N·m).

4. Three-phase input power / Humna galchaa faazii sadii

Pin = √3 × VL × IL × PF

VL = line voltage (V); IL = line current (A); PF = power factor. Motor output power is lower than input power because of losses.

VL = voolteejii sararaa (V); IL = yaa'a sararaa (A); PF = power factor. Humni ba'aa mootaraa kasaaraa irraa kan ka'e humna galchaa gadi ta'a.

05 • Worked example

Example: Calculate synchronous speed / Fakkeenya: saffisa shallaguu

Question / Gaaffii: A three-phase induction motor is supplied at 50 Hz and has 4 poles. Calculate its synchronous speed. / Mootarri induction faazii sadii 50 Hz fi poolii 4 qaba. Saffisa synchronous isaa shallagi.

Ns = (120 × f) / PNs = (120 × 50) / 4 = 1500 rpm

Answer / Deebii: Synchronous speed = 1500 rpm.

Important / Hubachiisa: Under normal motoring load, an induction motor's rotor speed is slightly less than synchronous speed because slip is needed to produce torque. / Yeroo hojii idileetti saffisni rotor mootara induction saffisa synchronous xiqqoo gadi ta'a; torque uumuuf slip barbaachisa.

Quick practice / Shaakala gabaabaa

A 50 Hz, 2-pole motor has what synchronous speed? / Mootarri 50 Hz, poolii 2 qabu saffisa synchronous meeqa qaba?

Show answer / Deebii ilaali

3000 rpm, because (120 × 50) / 2 = 3000 rpm.

06 • Safety first

Motor safety and maintenance / Nageenyaa fi suphaa

Electric motors can cause electric shock, burns, entanglement and mechanical injury. Only trained and authorized people should install, connect, test or repair motor circuits.

  • Isolate the supply before work; follow lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures.
  • Verify absence of voltage with a suitable, correctly rated tester.
  • Use appropriate PPE and follow applicable electrical safety rules.
  • Keep guards fitted over rotating shafts, couplings and fans.
  • Inspect unusual noise, vibration, overheating, smell and loose connections.
  • Follow the motor nameplate and manufacturer's instructions.

Mootaroonni elektirikaa balaa rukuttaa elektirikaa, gubaa fi miidhaa makaanikaa fiduu danda'u. Namoonni leenjii fi hayyama qaban qofa sarkiyuutii mootaraa dhaabuu, walqabsiisuu, qorachuu ykn suphuu qabu.

  • Hojii dura madda humnaa irraa adda baasi; tartiiba LOTO hordofi.
  • Meeshaa safartuu sirrii ta'een voolteejiin akka hin jirre mirkaneessi.
  • Meeshaalee ittisa dhuunfaa (PPE) fayyadami; seera nageenyaa hordofi.
  • Shaft, coupling fi fan naanna'an irratti haguuggii nageenyaa kaa'i.
  • Sagalee, raafama, ho'a garmalee, foolii fi walqabsiisa laafaa sakatta'i.
  • Gabatee maqaa mootaraa fi qajeelfama oomishtaa hordofi.

Never work on energized equipment. This lesson is educational and does not replace formal training, manufacturer documentation or applicable electrical codes.

Lesson summary / Cuunfaa barnootaa

  • A motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
  • The stator creates a magnetic field; the rotor turns and drives the load.
  • Induction motors are widely used in industry.
  • Synchronous speed depends on frequency and number of poles.
  • Isolation, LOTO, testing and guarding are essential for safe work.
  • Mootarri anniisaa elektirikaa gara makaanikaatti jijjiira.
  • Stator dirree maagneetii uuma; rotor naanna'ee meeshaa hojii sochoosa.
  • Mootaroonni induction industirii keessatti bal'inaan fayyadamu.
  • Saffisni synchronous firikuweensii fi baay'ina poolii irratti hundaa'a.
