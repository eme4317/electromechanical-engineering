Three-Phase Transformers

Construction, working principle, connections, calculations and safety

1. Learning objectives

After this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Define a transformer and explain electromagnetic induction.
  • Identify the main parts of a three-phase transformer.
  • Recognize star and delta connections.
  • Calculate turns ratio and three-phase apparent power.

Barnoota kana booda:

  • Tiraansfoormara ibsuu fi induction elektiroomaagneetawaa hubachuu.

  • Walqabsiisa Star fi Delta beekuu.
  • Reeshoo marsaa fi humna mul'ataa shallaguu.

2. What is a transformer?

A transformer is a static electrical device that transfers AC electrical energy from one circuit to another through a changing magnetic field. It can increase or decrease voltage while the frequency remains the same.

A three-phase transformer is used in three-phase power systems, such as substations, factories and large commercial buildings.

Tiraansfoormarri meeshaa elektirikii socho'aa hin taane yoo ta'u, anniisaa elektirikii AC marsaa tokko irraa gara marsaa biraatti dirree maagneetawaa jijjiiramuun dabarsa. Inni volteejii ol kaasuu ykn gadi buusuu danda'a; garuu frequency isaa hin jijjiiru.

Tiraansfoormarri faazii sadii buufataalee humnaa, warshaalee fi gamoowwan gurguddoo keessatti fayyada.

3. Main parts

PartFunction
Magnetic coreProvides a low-reluctance path for magnetic flux.
Primary windingReceives electrical energy from the supply.
Secondary windingDelivers transformed electrical energy to the load.
InsulationSeparates windings and live parts electrically.
Tank and cooling systemProtects the active parts and removes heat in oil-filled units.
BushingsProvide insulated terminals through the tank.

Qaamolee ijoo: Core (wiirtuu maagneetawaa), primary winding (marsaa galtee), secondary winding (marsaa ba'aa), insulation (adda baasaa), tank/cooling system (qodaa fi sirna qabbaneessaa), bushings (hidhaawwan insulaashinii).

4. Working principle

  1. AC current in the primary winding creates a changing magnetic flux in the core.
  2. The changing flux links the secondary winding.
  3. By electromagnetic induction, an AC voltage is induced in the secondary winding.
  4. When a load is connected, current flows to the load.
  1. Yaanni AC marsaa primary keessa darbu flux maagneetawaa jijjiiramu uuma.
  2. Flux jijjiiramu sun marsaa secondary waliin walitti hidhata.
  3. Induction elektiroomaagneetawaan volteejii AC marsaa secondary keessatti uuma.
  4. Yeroo load walqabsiifamu, yaanni elektirikii gara load ni darba.

5. Voltage and turns ratio

For an ideal transformer, voltage ratio is approximately equal to turns ratio:

V₁ / V₂ = N₁ / N₂

Where V₁ and V₂ are primary and secondary voltages; N₁ and N₂ are primary and secondary turns.

Step-down example

Given: V₁ = 11,000 V, V₂ = 400 V.

Ratio = 11,000 / 400 = 27.5

The primary voltage is 27.5 times the secondary voltage.

Afaan Oromo

Tiraansfoormara step-down keessatti volteejii ba'aa volteejii galtee caalaa gadi-aanaa dha. Fakkeenya kana keessatti 11,000 V gara 400 Vtti gadi bu'a.

6. Star and delta connections

Star (Y)

One end of each phase winding is connected to a common neutral point. A neutral conductor can be available.

VL = √3 × Vph

Delta (Δ)

The three phase windings are connected end-to-end in a closed loop. There is no neutral point in a standard three-wire delta connection.

VL = Vph

Star (Y): Xumuriin marsaa sadii bakka giddugaleessaa tokko irratti walitti hidhamu; neutral argachuun ni danda'ama.

Delta (Δ): Marsaaleen sadii walitti aananii marsaa cufamaa uumu. Delta sadarkaa sadii keessatti neutral hin jiru.

7. Three-phase apparent power

S = √3 × VL × IL

S = apparent power in volt-amperes (VA), VL = line voltage (V), IL = line current (A).

Worked example

A three-phase transformer supplies 400 V line voltage and 50 A line current.

S = 1.732 × 400 × 50 = 34,640 VA ≈ 34.6 kVA

Fakkeenya: volteejii line 400 V fi yaa'insa line 50 A yoo ta'e, S = 1.732 × 400 × 50 = 34.6 kVA.

8. Applications and maintenance

Applications: power transmission and distribution, industrial plants, commercial buildings, renewable-energy systems and isolation applications.

Basic maintenance checks: inspect for oil leaks, unusual noise, overheating, damaged bushings, loose connections and abnormal readings. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance plan.

Fayyadama: dabarsaafi raabsa humnaa, warshaalee, gamoowwan daldalaa fi sirna anniisaa haaromfamuu keessatti.

Sakatta'iinsa suphaa: dhangala'aa zayitaa, sagalee hin baramne, ho'a garmalee, bushing miidhame, hidhaa laafaa fi safartuu hin baramne ilaali. Karoora oomishtuu hordofi.

9. Electrical safety

  • Only qualified and authorized personnel should work on transformer equipment.
  • Isolate all sources, apply lockout/tagout (LOTO), and verify absence of voltage before work.
  • Follow site procedures, use appropriate PPE, and maintain safe approach distances.
  • Never open or touch energized equipment.

Meeshaa tiraansfoormaraa irratti hojjechuu kan qabu nama leenjii fi hayyama qabu qofa. Madda humnaa hunda adda baasi, LOTO hojiirra oolchi, hojii dura volteejii akka hin jirre mirkaneessi. PPE fi qajeelfama bakka hojii hordofi.
